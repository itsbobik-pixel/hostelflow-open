        Common questions

        How do I register a guest?

        Go to Check-in and add a photo of the passport or ID. HostelFlow reads it. Check the details, press Test (nothing is sent yet), then Send. The guest leaves the list once the Questura has them.

        The connection check says the Web Service key is wrong

        The key changes every time the Alloggiati Web password changes. Sign in to Alloggiati Web → your user name (top right) → Chiave Web Service, copy the new key into Settings → Police registration and save.

        A document can’t be read, or a field is wrong

        Take the photo again in good light, flat, without reflections, the whole page in view. You can always correct any field by hand before sending.

        How do I put HostelFlow on my phone?

        Pick your browser and follow the steps (with pictures of where to tap). On Android and computers it\'s often just one tap.

        Also in the menu under your name (top right) → Add app to home screen, or on hostelflow.it/install.

        How do I add my team?

        Settings → Team → “Add team member” and type their name. They then pick their name from the name tags at the top right. Under Access, switch on what they can change; everything else they can only view.
