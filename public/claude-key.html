Connect your Claude account

HostelFlow reads guests' documents with Claude. With your own Claude account you pay Anthropic directly — about a cent a scan — and there is no scan limit. It takes about 5 minutes.

  1. Sign up

    Go to platform.claude.com and sign up with Google or your e-mail.

  2. Buy credit

    Open Billing and press Buy credits. Add your card and buy $5 — enough for several hundred scans.

  3. Turn on auto-reload

    On the same page press Set up auto-reload, choose the amounts (for example: when it reaches $5, bring it back up to $15) and turn it on. Then the credit never runs out in the middle of a check-in.

  4. Create a key

    Open API keys and press Create key. A window about “identity federation” comes first: press Continue with an API key.

  5. Name it and set it to never expire

    Name: HostelFlow. Expires: Never. Then press Create key and copy the key that appears (it starts with sk-ant-).

    Important: choose Never. Otherwise the key stops after 30 days and scans stop working. The key is shown only once — copy it straight away and don't share it.

  6. Paste it in HostelFlow

    In HostelFlow open Settings → Check-in scan, paste the key and press Connect. Done — scans now use your own Claude credit.

Credit and auto-reload can be changed any time in Billing. Questions? Ask Flowy in HostelFlow, Settings → Help & support.
